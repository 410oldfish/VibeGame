# Game-icons.net status icons

The status icons under `Assets/Resources/UI/StatusIcons` are adapted from
[Game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net/) and used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Authors:

- Lorc: `muscle-up`, `shield-reflect`, `wingfoot`, `book-cover`, `aura`,
  `energy-breath`, `drop`, `embrassed-energy`, `arrow-dunk`, `thorny-vine`,
  `angel-wings`, `sprint`, `poison-bottle`, `broken-shield`, `despair`,
  `lightning-frequency`, `maze`, `vomiting`, `static`, `love-song`,
  `crossed-swords`, `vine-whip`, `burning-embers`, `cracked-shield`,
  `shattered-glass`, `snowflake-1`, `snail`, `ice-cube`, `fire-ring`, and
  `hammer-drop`.
- Delapouite: `chili-pepper`.

Project-local filenames describe their gameplay meaning. Some source icons are
reused for related effects. Color tinting is applied at runtime to distinguish
buffs from debuffs.
